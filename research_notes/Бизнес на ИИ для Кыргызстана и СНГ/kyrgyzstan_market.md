# Kyrgyzstan: market conditions for AI/LLM products (2025–2026)

Research date: 2026-10-07. Note: the Firecrawl search tool hit rate limits (HTTP 429) partway through, and kaktus.media and arxiv.org were blocked for direct fetch. A few questions therefore have thinner coverage, and these are listed under Gaps.

## 1. Economy & digital basics

### Takeaway
Kyrgyzstan is a small market (about 7.3M people), poor but growing fast: GDP grew more than 10% in 2025 and reached about 1.8 trn som. It is heavily mobile-connected, with about 88.5% internet penetration and 11.7M mobile connections. Instagram is the dominant social/commerce platform, and labour-migrant remittances (about $3.5bn in 2025) are a major income source.

### Cited Findings
- Population 7.32M (Oct 2025); 38.7% urban, 61.3% rural — [DataReportal Digital 2026: Kyrgyzstan](https://datareportal.com/reports/digital-2026-kyrgyzstan)
- Internet users 6.48M (Oct 2025), 88.5% penetration; 11.7M cellular mobile connections (late 2025) — [DataReportal 2026](https://datareportal.com/reports/digital-2026-kyrgyzstan)
- Median mobile download speed 52.09 Mbps; fixed 82.00 Mbps (end 2025) — [DataReportal 2026](https://datareportal.com/reports/digital-2026-kyrgyzstan)
- Social media user identities: 3.30M (Jan 2025, 45.6% of pop.) → 3.90M (Oct 2025, 53.3% of pop.; 60.2% of internet users) — [DataReportal 2025](https://datareportal.com/reports/digital-2025-kyrgyzstan); [DataReportal 2026](https://datareportal.com/reports/digital-2026-kyrgyzstan)
- Ad reach, late 2025: Instagram 3.90M; Facebook 938K; Threads 538K; LinkedIn 350K members; Messenger 159K; Reddit 152K; X 68.8K. DataReportal's page summary did not give YouTube or TikTok figures — [DataReportal 2026](https://datareportal.com/reports/digital-2026-kyrgyzstan)
- GDP Jan–Sep 2025: 1 trn 240.5 bn som, +10% y/y (Natstatcom) — [Economist.kg, 15.10.2025](https://economist.kg/ekonomika/2025/10/15/vvp-kyrghyzstana-vyros-na-10-za-ghod-i-prievyshaiet-1-2-trln-somov/)
- Full-year 2025 GDP about 1.8 trn som, growth >10%, according to Cabinet Chairman Adylbek Kasymaliev — [Economist.kg, 29.12.2025](https://economist.kg/ekonomika/2025/12/29/kasymaliiev-vvp-kyrghyzstana-dostigh-1-8-trln-somov-rost-bolieie-10/)
- Government target: GDP of $30bn by 2030 — [Economist.kg, 30.10.2025](https://economist.kg/ekonomika/2025/10/30/vvp-kyrghyzstana-sostavit-30-mlrd-plany-kabmina-k-2030-ghodu/)
- Gross remittance inflow in 2025 was $3.5bn, +16.8% y/y (National Bank data) — [Kabar](https://ru.kabar.kg/news/denezhnye-perevody-v-kyrgyzstan-rost-zavisimost-i-skrytye-riski-ekonomiki/)
- Remittances were about 14% of GDP in 2024. Without them, poverty would reportedly rise from about 9%. This comes from a weak source (an Instagram post, original source unclear) — [Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/p/DHC8AvLIvh6/?hl=en)
- Average nominal gross wage in 2025 was 44,419 som/month — [Wikipedia RU "Экономика Кыргызстана"](https://ru.wikipedia.org/wiki/%D0%AD%D0%BA%D0%BE%D0%BD%D0%BE%D0%BC%D0%B8%D0%BA%D0%B0_%D0%9A%D1%8B%D1%80%D0%B3%D1%8B%D0%B7%D1%81%D1%82%D0%B0%D0%BD%D0%B0) (secondary source)
- Trading Economics shows GDP per capita of $1,391 (Dec 2024). This figure is probably in constant prices, so it understates the nominal value — [Economist.kg / TradingEconomics via search](https://ru.tradingeconomics.com/kyrgyzstan/monthly-gdp-yoy)

### Inferences
- Nominal GDP per capita in 2025 is probably around $2,500–3,000. This is my rough calculation: 1.8 trn som at about 87 KGS/USD gives about $20–21bn, divided by 7.3M people. The average wage is about $500/month. Consumer willingness to pay for a $20/month subscription (ChatGPT/Claude) is therefore low. B2B, or products priced at $3–10/month in som, are more realistic.
- Instagram reach equals the whole social-media audience (3.9M), so Instagram is the main channel for SMB marketing and selling. Facebook is secondary.
- 61% of the population is rural, so audiences outside Bishkek and Osh are more Kyrgyz-speaking and mobile-only.

### Gaps
- No verified count of SMEs or individual entrepreneurs (ИП) for 2024–2025 was found, and no recent official count of labour migrants (common estimates are 700K–1M+, but I did not verify any source).
- No sector breakdown of GDP (trade, garment, tourism, agriculture shares) was retrieved.
- Official World Bank GDP-per-capita figures for 2024/2025 were not retrieved because of the rate limit.

## 2. IT sector: High Technology Park (HTP/ПВТ), startups, AI players

### Takeaway
HTP is the country's main IT vehicle. Its tax regime is near-zero: 0% profit tax, 0% VAT, 0% sales tax, 5% personal income tax, a reduced social-contribution base, and a 1% revenue fee to HTP. The regime has been indefinite since late 2024. In 2025, residents had about 17.1bn som (about $195M) in revenue, mostly from exports. The first Kyrgyz-language LLM, AiRUN, came out of the HTP ecosystem in October 2025.

### Cited Findings
- HTP tax regime: 0% profit tax, 0% VAT, 0% sales tax; 5% personal income tax for employees; social contributions of 12% calculated on the average salary rather than actual pay; 1% of revenue paid to the HTP directorate; at least 80% of revenue must come from exports (an aggregator gives conflicting thresholds for the IT-activity share: 90% versus 70% from 2024). The regime became indefinite at the end of 2024 (previously valid until 2026) — [kgaccount.com (incorporation-services aggregator, updated 27.09.2026)](https://kgaccount.com/%D0%BF%D0%B2%D1%82-%D0%B2-%D0%BA%D0%B8%D1%80%D0%B3%D0%B8%D0%B7%D0%B8%D0%B8/)
- 2024: 477 resident companies (383 in 2023); revenue 11.4bn som — [24.kg](https://24.kg/ekonomika/323990_vyiruchka_rezidentov_parka_vyisokih_tehnologiy_dostigla_114_milliarda_somov/); revenue $130.3M, 94% of it exports — [Aleminform](https://aleminform.kz/novosti/rezidenty-parka-vysokih-tehnologij-kr-dostigli-vyruchki-1303-mln/)
- 2025: revenue 17.1bn som (about $195.4M); "463 resident companies at end of 2025" — [kgaccount.com](https://kgaccount.com/%D0%BF%D0%B2%D1%82-%D0%B2-%D0%BA%D0%B8%D1%80%D0%B3%D0%B8%D0%B7%D0%B8%D0%B8/); HTP's own post confirms 17.1bn som — [HTP Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/p/DUpnfehDWey/). **Conflict:** 463 residents at end-2025 is lower than the 477 reported for 2024 by 24.kg. Either residents were removed or one figure is wrong.
- HTP residents export to 63 countries, mainly Europe and Asia — [Limon.kg](https://limon.kg/ru/news:83450)
- HTP resident exports to the USA were $51.4M in 2025 (vs $29.3M in 2024), out of about $200M total. The source is a Facebook post by Azis Abakirov, a former HTP director; it is not an official release — [Facebook](https://www.facebook.com/azis.abakirov/posts/10163952088974525/)
- HTP resident employees: 2,417 (2023), about 3,000 (2024/25) per an aggregator. This looks low and possibly counts only some categories — [kgaccount.com](https://kgaccount.com/%D0%BF%D0%B2%D1%82-%D0%B2-%D0%BA%D0%B8%D1%80%D0%B3%D0%B8%D0%B7%D0%B8%D0%B8/)
- AiRUN was presented on 28.10.2025 as the best LLM for Kyrgyz. It was trained on more than 58.8M words of Kyrgyz text and evaluated on BoolQ, HellaSwag and PIQA. It is claimed to beat international models on Kyrgyz and to reach 93–95% factual accuracy as a base for state AI systems. CEO: Chyngyz Arziev. Announced via HTP. Target uses: e-gov voice interfaces, call-center AI operators, voice assistants for banks and telecom — [Kabar](https://ru.kabar.kg/news/kyrgyzstan-predstavil-samuyu-kachestvennuyu-yazykovuyu-model-dlya-kyrgyzskogo-yazyka-airun/)
- A National Council on AI has been created. A National AI Strategy was described as "to be approved soon", aimed at local AI products. Kyrgyzstan has proposed creating a Regional AI Hub — [Kaktus via search](https://kaktus.media/541339); [Inform.kz](https://www.inform.kz/ru/kirgizstan-predlagaet-sozdat-regionalniy-hab-iskusstvennogo-intellekta-1f2cd3)

### Inferences
- An AI startup can incorporate as an HTP resident and pay almost no corporate tax, but the 80% export requirement makes HTP a better fit for products that sell into the CIS or globally than for purely domestic B2B. Check how the Digital Code (in force since Feb 2026) treats HTP, because it may have absorbed the HTP law.
- AiRUN is a domestic, government-friendly competitor for Kyrgyz-language voice and call-center use cases. Building on top of it or partnering with it may be smarter than competing with it.

### Gaps
- No list of other local AI startups was found beyond AiRUN. Known IT players such as Namba, Maxim/Mad Devs, Kaspi-like fintechs, or Codify/Ala-Too education were not verified in this session.
- No data on tech education (graduate numbers, bootcamps) was retrieved.

## 3. Payments

### Takeaway
Domestic payments are highly digital: QR payments through the national ELQR standard grew to 525M transactions worth 908.6bn som in 2025, and QR is accepted at more than 80K merchants. MBank is the QR leader. Taking international payments is harder: Stripe does not support Kyrgyzstan, and US sanctions on Keremet Bank (March 2025) show a real risk to correspondent and card access. Both Claude and ChatGPT/OpenAI API officially support Kyrgyzstan.

### Cited Findings
- QR payments in 2025: 525.1M transactions worth 908.6bn som, about 8x 2024 by count and 10x by value (National Bank data) — [Economist.kg, 03.02.2026](https://economist.kg/dengi/2026/02/03/qr-platiezhi-v-kyrghyzstanie-obnovili-riekord-dannyie-natsbanka/)
- QR payment accepted at more than 80,000 merchants as of 1 July 2025 — same source as above (via search summary)
- The Interbank Processing Center (ИПЦ, operator of the Elcart national card) has been the operator of QR payments since 2020 — [Economist.kg 2020](https://economist.kg/novosti/2020/03/02/mezhbankovskij-processingovyj-centr-stal-operatorom-po-provedeniju-v-kyrgyzstane-platezhej-cherez-qr-kody/)
- MBANK was recognized as the leader by number of active ELQR-payment users (Jan 2024) — [Economist.kg](https://economist.kg/novosti-kompanii/2024/01/29/mbank-priznan-lidierom-po-kolichiestvu-aktivnykh-polzovatieliei-elqr-platiezhiei/)
- Cross-border Kazakhstan–Kyrgyzstan QR payments were in the news in September 2026 — [Economist.kg 13.09.2026](https://economist.kg/dengi/2026/09/13/kazakhstan-kyrgyzstan-qr-platezhi/)
- Digital som: legal status granted in 2025; official rollout planned from 1 January 2027 — [Economist.kg (search summary)](https://economist.kg/dengi/2026/02/03/qr-platiezhi-v-kyrghyzstanie-obnovili-riekord-dannyie-natsbanka/)
- **Anthropic:** Kyrgyzstan is listed as supported for both the API and Claude.ai — [Anthropic Supported Countries](https://www.anthropic.com/supported-countries); [Claude Help Center](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/8461763-where-can-i-access-claude)
- **OpenAI:** Kyrgyzstan is listed as supported for ChatGPT and the API — [OpenAI Help: supported countries](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/5347006-openai-supported-countries-and-territories)
- **Stripe:** Kyrgyzstan is not a Stripe-supported country. The common workaround is to form a US LLC (e.g., via Stripe Atlas or a provider) — [Zenind guide](https://www.zenind.com/help/post/how-to-open-a-stripe-account-in-kyrgyzstan-a-complete-guide-for-entrepreneurs); [doola](https://doola.com/stripe-guide/how-to-open-a-stripe-account-in-kyrgyzstan)
- **Payoneer:** described as the more accessible option for Central Asian freelancers. Its specific support status for Kyrgyzstan was not confirmed from Payoneer's own site — [Zenind comparison](https://www.zenind.com/help/post/stripe-vs-payoneer-choosing-the-best-payment-solution-for-your-business)
- **Sanctions:** OFAC designated Keremet Bank in January 2025, alleging a sanctions-evasion scheme with Russia's Promsvyazbank. Sanctions took effect on 1 March 2025; the bank's Visa cards stopped working outside its own network from 28 February 2025. The UK also sanctioned the bank, and the National Bank of the Kyrgyz Republic petitioned for removal — [OCCRP](https://www.occrp.org/en/news/us-sanctions-kyrgyz-bank-over-alleged-russian-sanctions-violations); [Akchabar](https://www.akchabar.kg/en/news/sanktsii-v-otnoshenii-keremet-banka-ofitsialno-vstupili-v-silu-shest-dnej-nazad-rfdraqzxsocrwtfw); [Economist.kg](https://economist.kg/all/2025/01/22/kyrghyzstan-vyrazil-niedoumieniie-ssha-iz-za-sanktsii-protiv-kieriemiet-banka-gazprom-nieft-aziia-i-munai-myrza); [Times of Central Asia](https://timesca.com/kyrgyzstan-pushes-to-lift-u-s-sanctions-on-keremet-bank/)
- Russian guides on paying for ChatGPT recommend Visa/Mastercard cards issued in Kazakhstan, Armenia, Georgia "and other friendly countries", which implies that CIS (incl. KG) cards are a common workaround for Russians — [vc.ru](https://vc.ru/chatgpt/2769003-kak-kupit-podpisku-chat-gpt-v-rossii); [tproger](https://tproger.ru/articles/kak-oplatit-chatgpt-iz-rossii-v-2026-godu-rabochie-sposoby-opla)

### Inferences
- A local AI SaaS should charge domestic customers in som via QR/ELQR or MBank/O!Dengi links rather than by international card. Most SMBs and consumers live in the QR ecosystem.
- Because both Anthropic and OpenAI support KG, a local company can legally buy API access directly with a Visa/Mastercard from a non-sanctioned KG bank. This makes the "local reseller / wrapper with som billing and a Kyrgyz/Russian UX" model viable, and it can also serve Russian clients, subject to the provider's rules against resale to unsupported regions such as Russia ([Anthropic: restrictions for unsupported regions](https://www.anthropic.com/news/updating-restrictions-of-sales-to-unsupported-regions)). That is a significant compliance risk for any model built on serving Russia.
- Collecting payments from international customers requires a foreign entity (US LLC, UAE, etc.) or Payoneer, because Stripe is absent.
- KG banks face secondary-sanctions scrutiny for Russia-linked flows, so card acceptance by US merchants may be unstable for some banks.

### Gaps
- No verified current statements on whether cards from MBank, Optima, Bakai or Demir work for ChatGPT/Claude subscriptions (anecdotally Optima/Demir Visa cards work, but this is unverified).
- No user counts for MBank, O!Dengi or Elcart were found for 2025.
- Payoneer's official support for KG is unconfirmed.

## 4. Languages & Kyrgyz NLP

### Takeaway
Kyrgyz is a low-resource language. Frontier LLMs handle it unevenly, and natively authored benchmarks appeared only in 2025–2026: KyrgyzLLM-Bench evaluated 26 LLMs, and the local AiRUN model claims leadership. Russian remains the practical business language in Bishkek (see Gaps: no hard statistics retrieved).

### Cited Findings
- KyrgyzLLM-Bench (arXiv 2607.17173, 19 Jul 2026; authors Turatali, Turdubaeva, Izmailov, Alekseev, Nikolenko) is the first systematic large-scale LLM evaluation in Kyrgyz. It includes natively authored KyrgyzMMLU and KyrgyzRC plus post-edited WinoGrande, HellaSwag, BoolQ and TruthfulQA, and covers 26 open and closed models. Model rankings from English mostly carry over to Kyrgyz (WinoGrande, BoolQ, less so MMLU). HellaSwag shows a large English–Kyrgyz gap. Datasets and code are public — [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.17173)
- AiRUN Kyrgyz LLM (Oct 2025) — see section 2 — [Kabar](https://ru.kabar.kg/news/kyrgyzstan-predstavil-samuyu-kachestvennuyu-yazykovuyu-model-dlya-kyrgyzskogo-yazyka-airun/)
- A local engineer published research on making AI understand Kyrgyz as it is actually written online (typos, mixed script and language) — [Limon.kg](https://limon.kg/ru/news:86495)
- Enthusiast projects using AI to popularize Kyrgyz date back to at least August 2023 — [DW](https://www.dw.com/ru/popularizirovat-kyrgyzskij-azyk-pomozet-iskusstvennyj-intellekt/a-67215830)

### Inferences
- For B2B chatbots and voice bots, the product must handle Russian–Kyrgyz code-switching and Kyrgyz written in Latin-transliterated or typo-heavy form. This is a moat for local builders over generic global tools.
- Speech (ASR/TTS) in Kyrgyz is likely the weakest link and the biggest differentiator for call-center or WhatsApp voice-message automation.

### Gaps
- No hard statistics on Russian vs Kyrgyz use in business or government were found in this session (the 2022 census language data was not retrieved).
- No head-to-head scores for Claude, GPT or Gemini in Kyrgyz were extracted from KyrgyzLLM-Bench (the abstract page lacks per-model results).

## 5. Regulation & digital government

### Takeaway
Kyrgyzstan adopted a comprehensive Digital Code (No. 178, 31 July 2025), in force from 8 February 2026. It consolidates earlier IT laws, including personal and biometric data protection, and regulates AI and big data. Detailed obligations for AI systems still need to be read in the primary text. E-government is fairly advanced: 191 services run through Tunduk, and Tunduk handled 2.4bn transactions in 2025.

### Cited Findings
- The Digital Code of the Kyrgyz Republic, No. 178 of 31 July 2025, is in the official legal database — [Ministry of Justice CBD](https://cbd.minjust.gov.kg/3-48/edition/35412/ru)
- It enters into force on 8 February 2026 — [iakupbaeva.com legal blog](https://iakupbaeva.com/posts/rnanycpzd1-tsifrovoi-kodeks-kirgizskoi-respubliki-c); [Instagram reel](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DO45FssCHGv/?hl=en)
- It is described as the world's first comprehensive Digital Code. It consolidated five previously separate laws and regulates IT, data (including personal and biometric data) and AI — [Yur.kg, 13.12.2025](https://yur.kg/articles/kyrgyzstan-stal-pervoj-stranoj-v-mire-prinyavshej-czifrovoj-kodeks/)
- Ministry of Digital Development 2025 results: the Digital Code was the key event of the year and regulates AI, big data and digital services; 191 services available via Tunduk (141 fully automated); 2.4bn transactions in 2025; 33.7M documents in the "Infodocs" e-document system with 162K users; more than 1,000 civil servants trained in digital literacy — [Media Policy Institute (media.kg), 25.02.2026](https://media.kg/news/v-kyrgyzstane-byl-prinyat-czifrovoj-kodeks-i-zapustili-191-gosuslugu-cherez-tunduk/)
- AI is planned for Public Service Centers (ЦОНы) — [Kaktus](https://kaktus.media/doc/492715_v_conah_vnedriat_iskysstvennyy_intellekt.html)
- Kyrgyzstan was named the "digital center of the Turkic world" in 2025 — [Economist.kg, 29.05.2025](https://economist.kg/novosti/2025/05/29/kyrghyzstan-stal-tsifrovym-tsientrom-tiurkskogho-mira-ghlava-mtsr-ob-it-prieobrazovaniiakh-rieghiona/)
- Sanctions: see Keremet Bank in section 3.

### Inferences
- Any AI product processing personal data (CRM bots, call recordings, biometrics or voice) must comply with the Digital Code's data chapter. Founders should check for data-localization or registration requirements before sending data to US LLM APIs.
- Government AI procurement is likely to favor local or "sovereign" models (AiRUN) for e-gov.

### Gaps
- Specific AI provisions of the Digital Code (risk classes, labeling, liability), personal-data localization rules and penalties were not extracted, because the primary text was not parsed.
- Whether the National AI Strategy was formally adopted by October 2026 is unconfirmed.

## 6. Business platforms & SMB selling behaviour

### Takeaway
SMBs sell mainly through Instagram, which reaches 3.9M people, and through messengers (WhatsApp, Telegram — no figures retrieved). Wildberries and Ozon are significant export channels for the garment sector. More than 10K Kyrgyz sellers were on Wildberries by mid-2023.

### Cited Findings
- Instagram ad reach was 3.90M in late 2025, equal to the total social-media audience — [DataReportal 2026](https://datareportal.com/reports/digital-2026-kyrgyzstan)
- Kyrgyz sellers on Wildberries: 7,800 in 2022, 10,141 by H1 2023 (2.4x growth). Top categories: clothing, accessories, food, cosmetics, household goods. Sales of Kyrgyz goods exceeded 12bn som in 2022 — [Economist.kg 22.03.2023](https://economist.kg/novosti/2023/03/22/prodazhi-tovarov-iz-kyrgyzstana-na-platforme-wildberries-prevysili-12-mlrd-somov-v-2022-godu/); [Retail.ru](https://www.retail.ru/news/prodazhi-produktsii-iz-kyrgyzstana-na-wildberries-vyrosli-do-9-2-mlrd-rubley-s-nachala-goda/)
- Ozon opened its first pickup points in Kyrgyzstan in December 2022 — [Economist.kg](https://economist.kg/novosti/2022/12/29/v-kyrgyzstane-vpervye-otkrylis-punkty-marketplejsa-ozon-adresa/)

### Inferences
- The SMB customer-service workflow is "Instagram post → DM / WhatsApp → manual reply → QR payment". AI auto-replies in Instagram DMs and WhatsApp in Russian and Kyrgyz, with a QR payment link, is a clear wedge.
- Garment sellers on Wildberries and Ozon need product-card generation (Russian SEO text, photos) and responses to reviews. These are LLM-friendly tasks with a CIS-wide market.

### Gaps
- No 2024–2026 data was found for WhatsApp or Telegram penetration, 2GIS use, Lalafo traffic or listings, or current Wildberries/Ozon seller counts in KG.
- No survey of SMB pain points specific to KG was found.

## 7. Pain points AI could address (synthesis)

### Takeaway
Direct evidence on SMB pain points was not found. The items below are inferences from the facts above, not surveyed data.

### Cited Findings
- Rapid QR digitalization (525M QR transactions in 2025) — [Economist.kg](https://economist.kg/dengi/2026/02/03/qr-platiezhi-v-kyrghyzstanie-obnovili-riekord-dannyie-natsbanka/); AiRUN's targeted uses (call-center AI operators, voice interfaces for banks, telecom and e-gov) — [Kabar](https://ru.kabar.kg/news/kyrgyzstan-predstavil-samuyu-kachestvennuyu-yazykovuyu-model-dlya-kyrgyzskogo-yazyka-airun/)

### Inferences
- Likely high-value AI uses: (1) Instagram/WhatsApp/Telegram sales bots with Russian–Kyrgyz code-switching; (2) Kyrgyz-language voice and call-center automation for banks, telecom, MFIs and clinics; (3) marketplace content tools for Wildberries/Ozon garment sellers; (4) accounting and tax helpers for individual entrepreneurs (ИП) adapting to new rules (e.g., the Digital Code); (5) services for migrants and families: remittance-related information, Russian-language document help, and job and legal Q&A; (6) tourism: multilingual booking and concierge bots.
- Constraints: low ability to pay (average wage about $500/month), som-only payments, small total market (7.3M people). The HTP export incentive pushes a KG-based AI company to target Kazakhstan, Uzbekistan and the wider CIS as well.

### Gaps
- No primary data (surveys or reports) on SMB digital adoption, CRM usage or customer-service costs in Kyrgyzstan.
